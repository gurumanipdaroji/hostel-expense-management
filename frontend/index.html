How Shared Hostel Expenses Work

Two clear payment sources designed for realistic student living

TYPE 1

Common Money

At the start of the month, each member contributes equally (e.g. 5 students × ₹2,000 = ₹10,000) held by the Budget Holder.

  • Paid directly by the budget holder for hostel essentials.
  • Directly reduces the Current Common Balance.
  • System strictly prevents expenses greater than available common funds.
Example: Student A (Budget Holder) pays ₹1,500 for Rice.
₹10,000 − ₹1,500 = ₹8,500 Common Balance Remaining
TYPE 2

Personal Pocket Money

Any student can purchase things for the hostel using their own personal money on the spot.

  • Common balance DOES NOT decrease.
  • System records amount owed to that student for reimbursement.
  • Budget holder settles each student's amount during monthly closing.
Example: Student B spends ₹800 from own pocket for Vegetables.
Common Balance stays ₹8,500 | B will receive ₹800 at settlement
Transparent Monthly Settlement

Never confuse remaining common money with personal reimbursements owed. At the end of the month, see starting fund, common spent, remaining balance, and an exact student reimbursement checklist.
